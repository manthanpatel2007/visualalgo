Bubble Sort

Repeatedly steps through the array, comparing adjacent elements and swapping them if they are in the wrong order.

How it works

Bubble Sort compares neighboring elements and swaps them whenever they are in the wrong order. After each pass, the largest remaining element moves toward the end of the array.
